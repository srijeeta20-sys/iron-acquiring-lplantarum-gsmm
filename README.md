# iron-acquiring-lplantarum-gsmm
Genome-scale metabolic modelling (FBA, knockout analysis, FVA) of Lactiplantibacillus plantarum WCFS1 to identify the transporter bottleneck behind its clinically observed enhancement of human iron absorption. Predicts that 2x FE2abc overexpression yields a 50% growth improvement, matching the clinical benchmark reported by Hoppe et al. (2017).
